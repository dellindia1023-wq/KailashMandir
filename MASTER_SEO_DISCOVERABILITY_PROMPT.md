Create or improve content, metadata, structure, internal linking, and entity signals so that the site achieves:
- strong Google search rankings
- high eligibility for AI Overviews and AI-generated answers
- strong discoverability in conversational and answer-based search
- improved topical authority and entity recognition
- better trust, accuracy, and usefulness for users and search systems

## Mandatory optimization principles
1. Topical Authority
   - Build content around clear, high-value topic clusters.
   - Cover the main topic deeply and connect it to related subtopics.
   - Show expertise by organizing information around user intent, not just keywords.

2. Semantic SEO
   - Use semantically related phrases, variants, and question-based language.
   - Optimize for search intent, not just exact-match keywords.
   - Include natural variations such as timings, schedule, booking, online puja, festival dates, temple visit guide, and related spiritual queries.

3. Entity SEO
   - Clearly identify and reinforce the primary entity and supporting entities.
   - Use consistent names, aliases, locations, categories, and relationships.
   - Connect the brand to place, services, offerings, people, and events.

4. Content Graph
   - Build a strong content graph where each page supports and reinforces related pages.
   - Ensure each article has a clear role in the broader information architecture.
   - Group content by topic clusters and support each cluster with pillar pages and detailed articles.

5. Internal Link Graph
   - Add meaningful internal links between related pages.
   - Use descriptive anchor text that reflects the target page topic.
   - Link from high-authority pages to supporting pages and vice versa.
   - Avoid random links and ensure every link has a purpose.

6. E-E-A-T / Trust Signals
   - Demonstrate experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness.
   - Include factual, verifiable, and helpful information.
   - Avoid fluff, speculation, or generic filler.
   - If facts are uncertain, say so clearly rather than guessing.

7. Programmatic SEO
   - Where appropriate, create scalable pages for variations such as city, service, timing, category, festival, or question-based queries.
   - Ensure each page is unique, useful, and not thin or repetitive.
   - Use templates carefully while preserving quality and intent matching.

8. Knowledge Graph and Structured Data
   - Make the site easy for machines to understand.
   - Use schema markup for Organization, WebSite, LocalBusiness, Place, FAQPage, Article, BreadcrumbList, Event, and HinduTemple where relevant.
   - Ensure entities, attributes, and relationships are consistent.

9. AI Search Optimization
   - Write in a way that is clear, direct, factual, and structured.
   - Provide concise answers first, then add depth and context.
   - Use clear headings, bullet points, FAQs, summaries, and definitions.
   - Optimize for both traditional search and conversational answer systems.

## Writing and content requirements
- Write for humans first, but structure the content for machines second.
- Prefer clear, useful, specific content over generic promotional text.
- Use short paragraphs, scannable headings, and well-organized sections.
- Include direct answers to likely user questions.
- Add FAQ sections where natural.
- Use plain language, but preserve authority and clarity.
- Avoid keyword stuffing and unnatural repetition.

## Quality guardrails
- Never invent facts, dates, timings, or claims that are not supported by reliable evidence.
- If information is missing, mark it as pending or note that verification is required.
- Do not produce generic “SEO filler” content.
- Focus on relevance, usefulness, and accuracy.
- Preserve the religious and cultural sensitivity of the topic.
