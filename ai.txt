AI / GEO guidance for this repo:

- Use server-side functions for any sensitive model calls (service role) to avoid leaking keys.
- Prefer Resend for transactional email delivery; keep templates in `supabase/functions/shared/email/templates`.
- For geolocation features, rely on client-provided lat/lng and reverse geocoding via a trusted provider.
- Keep a privacy policy entry if collecting locations or personal data.
